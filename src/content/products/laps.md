# Laps

**Track laps for a race by scanning QR codes.**

Laps lets a team of volunteers track a race where each racer has a QR code scanned every lap, instead of tallying laps by hand or by memory.

## How it works

Pick or create a race, then point a phone's camera at a racer's QR code to log a lap. The scanner is agnostic to what it's reading — any racer code gets recognized, and a race's own QR code can be shown and scanned by other volunteers' phones to join them into the same race.

A race summary shows every racer, their lap count, and when each lap was recorded, so organizers can see standings as the race happens rather than reconstructing them afterward.

Scanning works even with a spotty connection: laps are queued locally and synced once a connection is available, so volunteers in different parts of a course don't need reliable signal to keep scanning.
